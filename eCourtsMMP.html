What is eCourts Mission Mode Project?

The eCourts Mission Mode Project is an initiative of the Government of India under the National eGovernance Plan (NeGP), aimed at transforming the Indian judiciary through technology.

Launched by the Department of Justice and implemented by the eCommittee of the Supreme Court, the project enhances efficiency, transparency, and access in the judicial process. This includes online case status, e-filing, video conferencing, and more.

The eCourts platform provides citizens and lawyers a seamless digital interface to access court services, and supports judicial officers in faster case disposal.
