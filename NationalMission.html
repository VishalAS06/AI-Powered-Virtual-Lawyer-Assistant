About the National Mission for Justice Delivery

The National Mission for Justice Delivery and Legal Reforms was launched by the Government of India to improve justice delivery and reduce delays in the system. It aims to make the judicial system more accessible, responsive, and effective.

The mission focuses on four key areas: policy and governance reforms, human resource development, infrastructure development, and ICT enablement of courts. The goal is to ensure timely delivery of justice and enhance citizen satisfaction.

The Mission also emphasizes improving court administration, setting performance standards, and strengthening capacities at all levels of the judiciary.
